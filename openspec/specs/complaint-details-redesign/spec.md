## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Split View for Citizen Voice and AI
The system SHALL visually separate the raw "Citizen Voice" from the "AI Analysis" to tell the storytelling of data transformation.

#### Scenario: User views complaint details
- **WHEN** the complaint details page is loaded
- **THEN** there is a clear visual distinction (e.g., side-by-side or distinct top/bottom blocks) between what the citizen wrote and what the AI extracted.
